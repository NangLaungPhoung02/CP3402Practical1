# Week 6

## Learning Activities & Resources

This week our team focused on setting up the group for the software development project. We formed a team of **four members** and discussed how we would work together throughout the semester. We decided to use **Discord** as our main communication platform because it allows us to quickly share updates, discuss problems, and arrange meetings. We also created our GitHub repository and project board to organise tasks and track our progress.

During our first meeting, we introduced ourselves, discussed the project requirements, assigned initial roles, and shared our ideas for the project. We talked about everyone's strengths and agreed on how we would divide the work fairly. We also discussed how often we should meet and decided to communicate regularly through Discord whenever someone completed a task or needed help.

---

## Estimated Hours of Explicit Learning Activity

I estimated that I would spend around 30 minutes setting up the teamwork tools and attending our initial group meeting. However, I actually spent approximately around 30 minutes.

Besides attending the meeting, I spent additional time setting up GitHub, joining the repository, checking the project board, reading the project requirements carefully, and becoming familiar with our communication tools. We also discussed possible project ideas and how we could divide the work effectively. Although this preparation took longer than expected, it helped everyone understand the project and establish a good workflow for future development.

---

## Content Insights

This week's activities helped me understand the importance of project planning before starting software development. Instead of writing code immediately, we first needed to organise the team, decide on communication methods, and clearly assign responsibilities. I learned that successful software projects require good planning as well as technical skills.

I also gained a better understanding of how collaborative tools such as **GitHub** and **Discord** support teamwork. GitHub allows multiple developers to contribute to the same project while keeping track of changes, and Discord makes communication much faster than relying only on email or messaging applications. Having regular discussions also helped us clarify the project requirements and ensure that everyone understood the overall goal.

## Career / Employability / Learning Insights

This week gave me a better understanding of how software teams collaborate in professional environments. Working as part of a team requires effective communication, responsibility, and cooperation. I realised that keeping teammates informed about progress and discussing problems early can prevent misunderstandings and improve productivity.

I was excited to start this project because I enjoy working with my teammates and learning from different ideas and perspectives. During our discussions, everyone contributed suggestions, which helped us develop a clearer understanding of the project and increased my confidence in working as part of a development team.

I also learned that teamwork is not only about completing individual tasks but also about supporting each other, attending meetings, sharing knowledge, and solving problems together. These communication and collaboration skills will be valuable in my future career, where software development is usually completed by teams rather than individuals. Throughout this project, I hope to continue improving both my technical abilities and my teamwork skills while contributing positively to the success of our group.
