Common questions
How fast is delivery?
We offer island-wide delivery within 2 hours. Same-day orders placed before 8pm are guaranteed.
What's the minimum order?
No minimum for regular orders. Free delivery on orders above S$80. Below that, a S$8 delivery fee applies.
Do you do wholesale / bulk orders?
Yes! We work with restaurants, hotels, and corporate buyers. Contact us for pricing and lead times.
Can I customise a gift set?
Absolutely. Send us a message with your budget, occasion, and preferences — we'll curate something special.
