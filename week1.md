Learning Activities & Resources

This week I worked on building my website for the alcohol store project. I focused on design webpages and adding product pages, images, and styling. I also searched online tutorials and guides to help me understand how to correctly link images and fix layout issues.

Estimated Hours of Explicit Learning Activity

I estimated that I would spend around 5 hours of my own time this week on this subject,because it just about only front web, not including time I spent on the assignment. However, in reality, I spent approximately 2 full days working on the project.

A large portion of this time was spent troubleshooting issues, especially with uploading and displaying images correctly on my website. I encountered problems with file paths, image folders, and CSS styling, which caused the images not to appear as expected. I had to repeatedly test, fix, and recheck my code to resolve these issues. In addition, I faced other technical problems while setting up pages and ensuring everything worked properly. Although this took much longer than I originally expected, it helped me gain more practical experience in debugging and understanding how different parts of a website are connected.

Content Insights

Through working on this task, I developed a better understanding of how images are handled in web development, particularly the importance of correct file paths and folder structure. I learned that even a small mistake in the image path or file name can cause the image not to display, and debugging these issues requires careful checking of both HTML and CSS. I also improved my understanding of how CSS affects image display, including properties such as width, height, and object-fit. Initially, I expected images to automatically fit into the layout, but I realised that proper styling is required to ensure consistency across different sections of the website. but in this assignment i did not decorate much about the style just very simple one and most of desgin i finied in their specific page. In addition, I learned how different parts of a website are connected, such as how HTML structure, CSS styling, and file organisation all work together. This experience helped me become more confident in identifying and fixing errors, especially when things do not work as expected.

Career/Employability/Learning Insights

This week’s experience showed me that problem-solving and debugging are essential skills in web development. In real-world projects, issues do not always have immediate solutions, and developers often need to spend time testing and experimenting to find the cause of the problem. I also realised the importance of patience and persistence, as many of the issues I faced took a long time to resolve. Being able to stay focused and continue working through these challenges is an important skill for future employment in the IT field. Furthermore, this experience highlighted the value of self-directed learning. I relied on online resources, tutorials, and trial-and-error to solve problems, which is similar to how developers continue learning in professional environments. These skills will be useful in my future career, especially when working on larger projects or in a team setting where independent problem-solving is required.
